Tree Removal Quote Checklist

Use this guide to describe the job accurately, compare scopes instead of headline prices, and prevent avoidable surprises after the tree comes down.

Before requesting a quote

  • Photograph the complete tree, the visible problem and the route from the street.
  • Identify everything beneath the canopy, including roofs, fences, utilities and landscaping.
  • Decide whether you want the stump ground, logs removed and wood chips hauled away.
  • Ask for proof of insurance and a written scope before work begins.

Information to provide

Tree condition

Describe dead sections, cracks, cavities, storm damage, mushrooms, exposed roots or a recent change in lean.

Nearby structures

List the house, garage, driveway, retaining wall, pool, shed, fence and neighboring property within reach.

Site access

Measure narrow gates and note slopes, steps, septic areas, soft lawns and overhead wires.

Desired finish

State whether the price should include debris hauling, log removal, stump grinding, rake-out and lawn protection.
